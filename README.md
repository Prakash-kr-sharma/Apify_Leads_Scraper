
# India Building Materials Lead Finder

A B2B lead generation Actor for discovering building materials businesses across India.

## Target Categories

- UPVC Window and Door Manufacturers and Dealers
- Aluminium Window and Door Manufacturers
- Aluminium Fabricators
- Glass Suppliers and Manufacturers
- Toughened and Tempered Glass Suppliers
- Glass Door Manufacturers
- Wooden, WPC, PVC and Steel Door Manufacturers
- Window and Door Contractors
- Facade and Curtain Wall Contractors

## Features

- Pan-India business discovery
- Public business phone numbers
- Public business emails
- Business websites and addresses
- Duplicate removal
- Contact verification
- CSV and JSON exports

## Technology

- JavaScript
- Apify
- Crawlee
